# Learning Checkpoints

Before moving to the next phase, answer without reading solution code:

1. Why is `Decimal` safer than `float` for money?
2. Why should the service layer, not the route, own transition rules?
3. What is the difference between HTTP 400, 401, 403, 404, 409, and 422?
4. Why should database tests use isolated transactions?
5. What belongs in environment variables?
6. How do indexes help status filtering, and what do they cost?
7. How will you prevent an AI response from directly changing a dispute status?
8. How will you evaluate classification accuracy and handle low confidence?
